A Note From Me

I hope this little website helps you get closer to Allah and makes your Islamic journey a little more beautiful and meaningful.

I made this with the hope that, just like we learn little things as a kid and carry them with us as we grow, these small habits can become a natural and beautiful part of your everyday life.

Use the Daily Islamic Goals to build meaningful habits, keep up with your Salah through the Salah Tracker, and make the Quran a daily companion with the Quran Tracker.

Use the Good Deeds Counter to remind yourself that even the smallest good deed matters. Watch your journey grow through the Progress Star, and whenever you need words for your heart, come back to the Duas and make a little conversation with Allah.

This website is not about being perfect. It is about taking one small step every day.

A prayer, a page of Quran, a good deed, a moment of remembrance, a sincere dua — every little effort counts.

I hope that when you look back one day, you can see how all those little steps brought you closer to Allah.

May Allah make your journey easy, accept your efforts, forgive your mistakes, strengthen your heart, and keep you close to Him always.

With love and a prayer in my heart,
Isba Eman ♡
